# Navigation evaluation

Generated by `run_eval.py` (60 runs, seeds 1,2,3, 6 in parallel, 6.2 min wall time). Every number below comes from these runs; per-run values are in `eval_runs.csv`.

Task: drive from the dock (1.5, 1.5) to (10.2, 6.6) in the 12 x 9 m warehouse with the behaviour tree's GoTo mission. Noise `low` is the nominal sim noise, `high` is 3x every sensor error (odometry scale/bias/white noise, IMU, LiDAR). `cluttered` adds 12 random 0.3-0.6 m boxes that are not in the prior map used by the localiser and planner. ATE/RPE are EKF vs ground truth (RPE over 1 m segments). Smoothness is computed on the driven ground-truth path resampled every 5 cm; plan turning is the summed heading change of the first global plan. Time to goal is averaged over successful runs only.
